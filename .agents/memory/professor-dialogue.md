name: Ungraded professor access
description: Standing product requirement for conversational access throughout the oral course.
---

The professor must be available at any time, including during lectures and assessments. The student can speak literally with him or write a question instead; he speaks his replies aloud and written text is also available. This conversational help never counts toward points or credit. The professor can gently redirect questions that are completely unrelated to the course, but should otherwise provide relevant expertise.

**Why:** The user emphasized that dialogue with the professor is an extremely important feature and explicitly required both spoken and written questions without affecting scores.

**How to apply:** Keep conversation separate from scored assessment responses; do not make a student leave or lose their place in an ongoing lecture or test. Preserve mandatory ElevenLabs speech for every professor reply.
